# Visual Identity

## Core Emotion

The visitor should feel that countless different worlds coexist in the same universe.

The project celebrates diversity of creation rather than a specific genre.

Each world may have a radically different atmosphere, setting and gameplay while remaining connected to a shared journey.

The platform is not about a single world.

The platform is about the connections between worlds.

---

## Mood

Keywords:

- Discovery
- Diversity
- Creation
- Journey
- Curiosity
- Possibility
- Connection

The visitor should feel:

> "I wonder what exists in those other worlds."

rather than:

> "This is another game marketplace."

---

## Visual Concept

A neutral personal world exists at the center of the experience.

Around it orbit worlds created by other creators.

Each world may have a completely different identity:

- Fantasy
- Science Fiction
- Historical
- Horror
- Cute
- Post-Apocalyptic
- Experimental

The visual contrast between these worlds is intentional.

The aggregator does not promote a specific setting.

Instead, it promotes the coexistence of many settings.

---

## Hero Illustration Concept

```text
                      ☢
               Apocalypse World

                            🛸
                      Sci-Fi World


      🏰                                   🎀
Fantasy World                      Cute World


                    🌎
                Your World


                           👻
                     Horror World
```

The central world represents the traveler's personal world.

The orbiting worlds represent creations made by other creators.

The traveler moves between these worlds and brings back new possibilities.

The central world does not automatically evolve.

Creators decide themselves how their world changes and what they build with the possibilities they unlock.

## Logo Ideas

### Orbiting Worlds

```text
       ○

   ○   ●   ○

       ○
```

The central world represents the traveler's world.

The surrounding worlds represent destinations.

The logo communicates:

- Connection
- Diversity
- Travel

### Connected Planets

Several planets of different sizes and shapes connected by orbit lines.

The logo should communicate that worlds remain independent while being part of the same ecosystem.

## Visual References

Inspirations:

- Journey
- No Man's Sky
- Outer Wilds

Common themes:

- Exploration
- Curiosity
- Discovery
- Personal adventure

Avoid visual identities strongly associated with:

- Traditional fantasy
- Cyberpunk
- Historical simulation

The aggregator must remain welcoming to all genres.

---

## Design Principles

### Worlds Before Technology

The visitor should first notice worlds and possibilities.

Technical explanations come later.

### Fantasy Before Economy

The dream of traveling between worlds should be presented before discussing currencies, rewards and moderation systems.

### Creator Freedom

The platform provides possibilities.

Creators decide how to use them.

### Diversity as a Strength

A fantasy world and a science-fiction world should be able to coexist naturally.

The diversity of worlds is one of the platform's greatest assets.
