# Website Storyline

## Vision Statement

Our Connected Worlds is a network of creator-made worlds connected by a shared traveler.

You are not the hero of a single story.

You are a traveler crossing countless worlds, gathering resources, knowledge and memories from each journey until one day you create a world of your own.

The destination is not predefined.

The traveler decides when the journey ends.

---

# Section 1 - Hero

Title:

Our Connected Worlds

Subtitle:

Travel through worlds created by other players. Bring back what you discover. Build a world of your own.

Purpose:

Introduce the fantasy of the traveler.

The visitor should imagine their own journey immediately.

---

# Section 2 - The Traveler

Description:

Most stories begin and end in the same world.

Our Connected Worlds explores another idea.

What if every world was only one stop on a much larger journey?

A traveler begins with a small personal world and very few possibilities.

To grow, they must leave home.

Diagram:

```text
Your World
     ↓
Travel
     ↓
Another World
     ↓
Return Home
     ↓
Travel Again
```

Purpose:

Establish the player's role.

---

# Section 3 - The Personal World

Description:

Every player owns a personal world.

At first it is little more than an empty land waiting to be shaped.

As travelers explore, they unlock new possibilities to transform it.

The personal world is:

- A home
- A creation space
- A record of past journeys
- The starting point of future adventures

Purpose:

Create attachment to the player's home world.

---

# Section 4 - The Worlds

Description:

Every world is created by another creator.

Each one may define:

- Its own story
- Its own gameplay
- Its own challenges
- Its own rewards
- Its own rules

A world may be:

- A side story
- A sequel
- A dungeon
- A kingdom
- A survival challenge
- An entirely different genre

Purpose:

Show the freedom available to creators.

---

# Section 5 - Connected Journeys

Description:

A journey does not end when a world is completed.

Travelers bring back resources, items and progression that contribute to their personal growth.

Diagram:

```text
World A
     ↓
Discovery
     ↓
Traveler
     ↓
Personal World
     ↓
World B
```

Purpose:

Explain continuity between worlds.

---

# Section 6 - Become a Creator

Description:

Every creator began as a traveler.

As players gain experience and resources, they unlock more possibilities for their own creations.

One day a traveler may decide to stop searching for worlds and start building one.

Diagram:

```text
Traveler
      ↓
Explore Worlds
      ↓
Gain Experience
      ↓
Unlock Creation Tools
      ↓
Create World
```

Purpose:

Reveal the long-term progression.

This is one of the project's defining ideas.

---

# Section 7 - Creator Economy

Description:

Creators are rewarded when players choose to visit their worlds.

A successful world attracts visitors.

Visitors provide income.

Income unlocks new possibilities.

Diagram:

```text
Create World
      ↓
Players Visit
      ↓
Creator Earns Currency
      ↓
Improve World
      ↓
More Players Visit
```

Purpose:

Show how creators are encouraged to continue building.

---

# Section 8 - Moderation

Description:

New worlds enter a trial period before becoming fully approved.

The community helps identify:

- Fraud
- Misleading descriptions
- Abusive economies
- Illegal content

Purpose:

Maintain trust in the ecosystem.

---

# Section 9 - Technical Architecture

Description:

Worlds remain independent and isolated.

The aggregator manages:

- Accounts
- Progression
- Resources
- Economy

Games execute inside sandboxed environments and return validated rewards.

Purpose:

Demonstrate safety without distracting from the vision.

---

# Section 10 - Long-Term Vision

Description:

Imagine thousands of worlds created by thousands of creators.

Some worlds tell small stories.

Some tell epic stories.

Some exist only to experiment.

Together they form a universe of interconnected journeys.

The goal is not to become the hero of a single world.

The goal is to decide what kind of world your own journey will create.
