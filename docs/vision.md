# Our Connected Worlds
## Vision

Our Connected Worlds is a game aggregator connecting independent games through a shared progression, economy, and resource system.

Players own a personal world that acts as their home. Initially, this world is empty and contains only decorative tools.
To obtain new resources, abilities, and possibilities, players must travel to other worlds created by other creators.

The objective is to create continuity between gaming experiences while preserving the freedom of creators to build their own rules, stories, and gameplay. Creators could then take advantage of it to connect their respectives games, making it possible for all kinds of collaborations.

## Core Principles
### Shared Progression

A player's progression exists at the aggregator level rather than inside a single game.

Progression can include:

- Character level
- Currency
- Unlocked resources
- Owned items
- Reputation

Any game integrated into the aggregator may choose to use all, part, or none of this progression.

### Personal World

Each player owns a personal world.

This world acts as:

- Home
- Inventory showcase
- Creation space
- Long-term progression area

Players can improve it by importing resources and items obtained from other worlds.

Initially, this world contains very few possibilities.

Exploration of other worlds is required to unlock more.

### Independent Worlds

Each creator can publish one or more worlds.

A world may define:

- Its own story
- Its own gameplay
- Its own rules
- Its own rewards
- Its own economy

The aggregator does not dictate how a game should be designed.

Its role is to provide common services and ensure compatibility.

### Player Loop

Personal World
>
Explore Available Worlds
>
Access a World
>
Play
>
Earn Resources
>
Import Resources
>
Use Ressources in Personal World or other compatible.


The player is encouraged to explore many worlds to enrich their own.

### Creator Loop

Create World
>
Publish World
>
Players Visit
>
Creator Earns Currency
>
Unlock New Creation Resources
>
Improve World
>
Attract New Players

The creator is rewarded when players choose to play their content.

### Shared Item Ecosystem

Items belong to a global aggregator catalog.

Every item has a unique identifier and a shared definition.

Examples:
Iron Ore, Crystal Shard, Ancient Relic, Fire Flower, etc.

World creators decide:

- How items are obtained
- Their rarity
- How they are used

When a player obtains an item and imports it into the aggregator account, it becomes available to other compatible worlds.

This creates continuity between experiences.

### Economy

The economy is based on the relationship between players and creators.

Players:

- Pay access fees
- Earn resources
- Progress their accounts

Creators:

- Earn money when players access their worlds
- Pay for rewards distributed in-game
- Invest earnings into improving their worlds

The system encourages creators to find a balance between accessibility and reward value.

### Moderation

New worlds enter a trial phase before being fully approved.

Goals:

- Detect fraud
- Detect abusive economies
- Detect misleading content
- Protect players

Players participating in trials act as judges.

A world becomes approved when enough players reach a strong consensus regarding compliance with platform rules.

## Security

Games are executed inside isolated environments.

The aggregator:

- Starts the game
- Provides shared resources
- Provides player data
- Receives rewards on session completion

The game returns a structured result describing:

- Currency gained
- Items obtained
- Progress achieved

The aggregator validates the result before applying rewards.

This prevents direct access to player data and limits abuse.

### Long-Term Goal

Create an ecosystem where:

- Players continuously develop a persistent identity.
- Creators continuously build new experiences.
- Players are pushed gently to creation side
- Worlds remain independent.
- Progression remains connected.

The value of the platform comes from the interaction between worlds rather than from a single game.
